About

Research at the intersection of markets and policy

I bring approximately 20 years of experience across derivatives markets, financial stability, regulation, fixed-income analytics, and academic research.

My work uses empirical asset pricing, financial econometrics, and derivatives-market data to study market risk, liquidity, volatility, margin policy, and benchmark reference rates. I have served at the Federal Reserve Board since 2011 and previously worked as a quantitative analyst at KIS Pricing in Seoul.
